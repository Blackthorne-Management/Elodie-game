# BLOODLINES: THE RACE TO ELODIE'S GRACE — SHARED ACTION DECK
### Full card list — 126 cards (71 Hand Cards, 6 of them Reactions / 55 Instant-Event Cards)

This is the complete draft deck referenced in Section 7 of the rulebook. Numbers are for reference/printing, not in-game card IDs. Effects are written tight and literal, ready to transcribe to physical cards as-is. Flag anything that needs rewording or rebalancing after a playtest.

---

## NOTES FOR BALANCING / PLAYTESTING

- **Elodie cards (10 total):** 81, 83, 84, 89, 90, 94, 95, 96, 98, 102 — mechanically unchanged from their base category, but carry Elodie's name, a short flavor line, and (for final print) a distinct card back or border so they visibly stand apart from an ordinary Instant draw. These are deliberately pulled from the Global Effects and Targeted categories, since those already read as "the world/fate acting on everyone" — reframing them as her direct will costs nothing mechanically.
- **Wealth support tally:** 13 dedicated Hand resource cards + 10 dedicated Instant cards, plus several Wealth-related effects scattered through the dice-chaos and targeted categories — comfortably the best-supported pillar, as intended given only 2 Trade tiles exist on the board.
- **Block/Deflect ratio:** 8 of 65 Hand Cards (~12%) — rare but not vanishingly so across a full game with reshuffles.
- **Ranged/Cursed attacks:** 5 of 65 Hand Cards — intentionally uncommon, since most combat should come from positioning and adjacency, not ranged sniping.
- A few cards reference "tie" resolution explicitly (91-100, 114-115) — these follow the general principle: a tie means no effect rather than picking a winner, to avoid table arguments over who's "really" in the lead.
- Card 19 (Market Day) and 108 (Stolen Secrets) both interact with the hand-size cap from the rulebook — double-check these read cleanly against Section 4's overflow rule when transcribed to final cards.
