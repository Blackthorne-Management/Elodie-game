# Questions for the objective reviewer

The owner wants the objective reviewer's view on these when reviewing Chapter 2. Each goes into the Chapter 2
reviewer brief, and each records the answer once a review gives one.

1. **Secret items (owner request, Oct 19).** Solange's Quill (Chapter 1) and Caona's Conch (Chapter 2) are planned
   in their own chapters as optional secret quests, instead of moving the Conch to Chapter 11 (where it would come at
   levels 25 to 28 with little left to use it on). Do the two items earn their place where they are? Background:
   `2026-10-19-chapter2-side-quests-outline.md` and its review (section D5).

   **Not built yet.** An earlier version of this file said they were built; they are not. `solange_quill` exists only
   as an item definition (`quest/src/data/rpg.ts`) and a sprite, with no dialogue, map or quest that gives it, and the
   Conch does not exist in `quest/src/` at all. `PLAN-ALL-CHAPTERS.md` puts the secret items after the release.

   **Answered by review 1 (Oct 21, `2026-10-21-review-1.md`, "The owner's question: the secret items"):** keep the
   Quill in Chapter 1 and the Conch in Chapter 2, and build them after the release, as the plan says. The Quill's
   revive earns its place from Chapter 4 or 5 (a short Chapter 1 puzzle, with reward text that does not promise more
   than a two-person party can use); build the Conch once there are two or more optional sea destinations after
   Chapter 2. Settled: no change to the plan.
