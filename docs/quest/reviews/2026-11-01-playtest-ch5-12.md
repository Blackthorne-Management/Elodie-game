Objective playtester pass, review only (no code changed). Driven with Playwright on the dev server, jumping with
`?at=<marker>` and pressing through scenes. Viewports: desktop 1280x800, phone portrait 390x844, phone landscape
844x390. Screenshots are in the reviewer's scratchpad,
`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/65f3c4a5-da26-5616-992a-6f5d1fe748a4/scratchpad/review-b/` (named `<marker>-<d|p|l>[-step].png`;

once, after the verdict). Those need a follow-up pass that plays those scenes through.

## Verdict

The story beats land and the chapter cards, voyages and Echo prompts work in every chapter. The big problems are all of
the kind the owner just found: **who is on screen**. Since the walking party became four, Yari and the other
companions still talk, get narrated and get stage directions from wherever they are not, and the four who do walk often
stand in a single-file column where two of them are completely hidden. Second: **the phone in portrait** shows the game
at about a fifth of the screen, with text a 12-year-old or a grandparent cannot read. Third: the card prompt says
"(A to continue)" on a keyboard, where A means "walk left".

## Findings, most harmful first

### 1. Party members talk, and are narrated and directed, while not on the map (systemic, Ch5 to Ch9, Ch10 landing)
- **Where:** every chapter 5 to 9 (Yari speaks but only the chapter's newcomer walks); `muster-landing` (Ch10).
  Desktop and phone.
- **What I saw:** `skarragol-d-c.png`: Yari's portrait and line ("The road goes down west to the fair") with Yari
  nowhere on the map (snapshot `yari: null`). Line counts of Yari speaking in the dialogue files: seat 28, skarragol 7,
  kuroshi 10, jwaladesh 9, aldermoor 3. Narration that needs her on screen: `skarragol.ts:771` "Azzy and Gianna walk
  away from the others ... Yari keeps Anissa with her." At `muster-landing` (`muster-landing-d-*.png`) Edmund, Samira,
  Vikram, Bayarmaa and Haru each speak ("Nine of us and a horse") and `act([{ do: 'look', actor: 'bayarmaa' }])` runs,
  with only Gianna, Anissa and Azzy on the map. This breaks the owner's "shown, not told" rule and is exactly the kind
  of thing the walking-party fix created.
- **Fix:** for any scene line spoken by a member who does not walk, have the stage borrow them: `enter` them from just
  off the party (one step behind, or walking up from the edge) before their first line and `exit` or sync them back at
  the scene's end (the `borrowed` mechanism already exists for Yari in `FieldScene.placeYari`). Add a content test:
  every `say(member, ...)` and every `act` naming a party member in a scene must have that member walking or staged in
  that scene. For pure ambient lines (party chatter), reassign them to a walker.

### 2. Single-file columns hide half the party (systemic, every chapter)
- **Where:** `aldermoor-assize` (Ch9, the courtroom), `skarragol`, `petitions`, `muster-council`, `jwaladesh-landing`,
  `chapter7-end`, `hearing-arrivals`, `claim-arrival`. Worst on the phone.
- **What I saw:** `aldermoor-assize-d.png`: at the Assize, Gianna 7,24, Anissa 7,23, Azzy 7,22, Edmund 7,21. Only
  Gianna and Azzy can be seen; Anissa is entirely behind Gianna, and Edmund is behind Azzy and looks like part of the
  lectern behind. `skarragol-d-c.png`: Anissa invisible between Gianna and Azzy. `concourse-d.png`: Samira stands on the
  tile below Gianna and covers her body. Sprites are about 1.5 tiles tall, so a follower directly below covers the one
  above, and a follower directly above disappears behind.
- **Fix:** when the party stops (scene start, arrival, after a door), fan the followers out sideways (left and right
  of Gianna, one row back) instead of straight behind, as `muster-quay` already looks (`quay.png`, a clean side-by-side
  line). In long vertical walks, offset the third and fourth walker by one column. Do not let a follower stop on the tile
  directly south of the player.

### 3. "(A to continue)" on keyboard means "walk left"
- **Where:** every chapter card and ending card (`FieldScene.ts:2167`), the item showcase ("(A or tap to continue)",
  `ItemShowcase.ts:217`). Desktop.
- **What I saw:** `chapter6-end-d.png`, `the-end-d.png`. On the keyboard, `KeyA` is bound to left
  (`input/keyboard.ts:14`); confirm is Z, Enter or Space. A grandparent on a laptop presses A and nothing happens. The
  Echo prompt already adapts ("Press E to Echo" on keyboard, "Press Echo to Echo" on touch), so the cards should too.
- **Fix:** use the same per-device label as the Echo prompt: "(Enter to continue)" on keyboard, "(A to continue)" on a
  gamepad, "(Tap to continue)" on touch. Also reword the touch Echo prompt: "Press Echo to Echo" reads like a typo;
  "Tap Echo" is enough.

### 4. Phone portrait: the game is a strip, and its text is unreadable
- **Where:** every screen, 390x844 (the target size in CLAUDE.md).
- **What I saw:** `menu-p-m0.png`, `bat-hale-p-b5.png`, `claim-count-p-b.png`: the play area is 390x~220 px between a
  large minimap above and the pad below; dialogue text is about 7 px tall; the Status menu and battle enemy names are
  about 5 to 6 px tall and cannot be read. "Turn your phone sideways for a bigger view" is shown, but small, in grey,
  under the game.
- **Fix:** in portrait, either scale the game canvas to fill the width at a larger zoom (fewer tiles across, 2x text),
  or make the rotate prompt a clear full-width message the first time (with a picture of a turning phone). At minimum,
  set a minimum font size for dialogue, menu and battle labels in portrait (around 12 px CSS), and shrink the minimap
  to give the game the space.

### 5. Battle: enemy name labels overlap each other and the wrong sprites
- **Where:** `flagship_hale` battle (the Ch11 boss), all viewports.
- **What I saw:** `bat-hale-d-b0.png`: four labels stacked diagonally ("Regent's Sealbearer", "Crown Marine A",
  "Crown Marine B", "Captain-General Hale"), each with its HP bar, overlapping one another and sitting across other
  enemies' bodies: "Captain-General Hale" lies over Crown Marine A. A child cannot tell which name is which figure. On
  the phone (`bat-hale-p-b5.png`) the labels are an unreadable smear.
- **Fix:** put each enemy's name and HP bar directly under its own feet (or in a fixed list in the right half of the
  bottom panel, which is empty on desktop until the command menu opens), never floating above another enemy. Highlight
  the targeted enemy's name in the list when choosing a target.

### 6. The "Next:" turn order overflows and truncates names
- **Where:** same battle, all viewports.
- **What I saw:** `bat-hale-d-b0.png`: "Next: Yari > Sealbear > Hale > Gianna > Samira > A" running off the right edge;
  "Sealbearer" cut to "Sealbear", the last name cut to "A". With 4 fighters and 4 enemies it already overflows; at 6

### 9. Archive disguise robes make Gianna and Anissa faceless

### 11. The Muster landing tells the harbor is full; the map does not show it
- **Where:** `muster-landing` (Ch10). Desktop.
- **What I saw:** narration "The Old Harbor is full of hulls that are not Zetwal's. A grey longship with a horse-tail
  standard. Two boats with paper lanterns. A ship with a brass lamp ... A Dorini merchantman with a green pennant."
  (`zetwalMuster.ts:91-93`) while the visible harbor (`muster-landing-d-a.png`, `-d-d.png`) shows none of them; the only
  candidate is a green three-square block by the pier. The ship objects exist (`muster_ship_*_pier`) but are not seen
  during these lines (wrong condition timing or off camera).
- **Fix:** make sure the five ships are visible when the narration runs (the condition true before the landing scene,
  and the camera panned across them, one per line, as the narration names each). Check that the ship drawings read as
  ships at a glance and not as square tiles.
- Fixed (Nov 1): the ships appear with the first line (the sync) and the camera crosses the harbor as each is named: the middle of the harbor, then the west piers (the longship and the lantern boats), then the east piers (the lamp ship and the Dorini merchantman), and back to the party for Edmund's line. The five hulls were tile placeholders (three coloured squares); they are now drawn as ships at three quarters size in each house's colours (grey longship with a white standard, lantern yellow, brass orange and gold, the green pennant, Zetwal gold), at the piers by day and in the dawn line. A new stage cue, `mark`, names an unseen spot the camera can look at (engine/stage.ts).

### 14. Ending and quest-reward pop-ups that repeat after a jump

- The battle shows whose turn it is (">Yari" in the list and a glow under the sprite, `bat-hale-l-b5.png`).
