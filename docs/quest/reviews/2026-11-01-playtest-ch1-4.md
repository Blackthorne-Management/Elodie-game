# Playtest review: Chapters 1 to 4 (Pòdlimyè, Xaraguá, Ardencia, Al-Doria)

Reviewer: objective playtester (Claude, Opus), Nov 1, 2026. Review only: no repo file was changed.

How it was played: the dev server (`npx vite --port 5311`), Chromium driven by Playwright, at desktop 1280x800,
phone portrait 390x844 and phone landscape 844x390 (touch overlay on). Jumps with `?at=` and `?intro=0`; walks
replayed from `routes/zetwal-act1.json`; fights played by hand (Attack, Skill, back with B) and by spamming Attack.
Played: the intro film and title, the opening and festival, the Cane Road Echo and boar fight, the lamp, the
Chapter 1 card and voyage, the Yucayeque pier fight, the proof scene, the caney breach and Cacique Anaco taken, the
night plaza, the occupied village and menu, the Thorne blockade fight (lost it with Attack only, saw the goddess
Game Over), Ardencia's arrival and gate, the Trial of Blades and its aftermath, the forge cell and forge Echo, the
Al-Doria quay and bazaar, and the guild-vault Echo. Not reached: the Azzy-joins scene itself (seen him at the
gate, in the trial and the cell), the bazaar majlis/appraisal puzzles, the vault balance and chest.

Screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/65f3c4a5-da26-5616-992a-6f5d1fe748a4/scratchpad/review-a/` (the
session scratchpad; copy any you want to keep). Paths below are file names in that folder.

## Verdict

The story beats land, the fights are readable at desktop size, the new acting-fighter mark and B-back work
(checked: target -> B -> commands), and the festival crowd no longer has twins. The problems a first-time player
will hit are mostly of the same family the owner just fixed: **people drawn on top of people**. Sprites are 32 px
tall on 16 px tiles, so anyone standing directly in front of or behind someone hides half of them, and the party
walks in a single-file column, so **Anissa is hidden behind Gianna in almost every scene of four chapters**,
including the ones that are about her. The second family is **battle text colliding**: enemy name labels over
other enemies, damage numbers on top of names, the turn queue cutting names and showing "B > A" (read as
buttons). Third: **two story moments told in narration only** (the boar, the burning stalls). Fourth: the phone
picture is small enough that dialogue and battle text are hard to read, and the prompts say "A" and "B" to
keyboard players, for whom the A key walks left.

## Findings, ranked by how much they hurt a first-time player

### 1. Anissa is hidden behind Gianna (and the party is a blob) in most scenes
- Where: everywhere the party stands still in a column. Lamp scene (`?at=lamp-unlit`, zetwal-temple), Ardencia
  arrival (`?at=ardencia`, ardencia-flats), the night plaza (`?at=attack`, xaragua-plaza), the Alcázar entry
  (`?at=gate`), the Al-Doria quay and gate (`?at=aldoria`), the vault door (`?at=vault-echo`). All viewports.
- What I saw: Anissa's head disappears behind Gianna's back; only her two puffs show above Gianna's hair, and the
  pair reads as one odd creature. At the lamp (the scene where light pours out of Anissa) she is a pink body under
  Gianna's head. On the Ardencia strand Gianna, Anissa and Yari stand in one column and Anissa is invisible. At
  the vault door Gianna and Azzy are one sprite with the guide arrow on Gianna's face.
  Screens: `lamp-d-04-talk10.png`, `lamp-d-05-talk15.png`, `ard-d-01-start.png`, `taken-d-20-talk45.png`,
  `taken-d-22-after.png`, `ard-d-18-gate.png`, `vault-d-01-start.png`, `mont-ald.png` (gate frames).
- Why it hurts: the owner's rule is that story moments are shown. The player cannot see Anissa react when the
  scene is about her, and a 12-year-old cannot tell who is where.
- Fix: when the party stops (after a walk, and at every scene start), have the follower step to the side (left
  or right of Gianna, whichever is open) rather than directly behind; in staged scenes, place Anissa beside
  Gianna, not in the tile behind her. Cheap safety net: draw the follower in front when she is directly behind,
  or give her a 1 px outline so her shape survives the overlap. A test can assert that at every `act` the
  follower is not on the tile straight above or below the player.

### 2. The boar never appears on the map
- Where: `?at=cane-road`, zetwal-road, walking to 9,12. Desktop.
- What I saw: "The cane rustles. Something heavy is moving through it, fast." / "A boar bursts onto the road,
  bristles up, tusks low, and plants itself between you and the gate." The road is empty in every frame; the
  battle starts from narration. Screen: `cane-d-13-talk3.png`.
- Why it hurts: the first fight of the game is told, not shown (owner rule, Oct 17).
- Fix: a boar map sprite (the battle art scaled, or a 24x24 walker) that shakes the cane tiles, charges out to
  the road between the party and the gate and stops; the lines play there. Same for any other first-encounter
  monster in Chapters 1 to 4.

### 3. The burning plaza is narrated over a calm daylight map
- Where: right after Cacique Anaco is taken, xaragua-plaza (`?at=attack` lands just after it). Desktop.
- What I saw: "Night. The stalls' thatch is burning. Crown drums beat on the dance ground, and red banners go up
  by the steps." The map is only slightly dimmed, green and bright; no fire, no smoke; banners are there. Screens:
  `taken-d-20-talk45.png`, `taken-d-21-talk48.png`, `taken-d-22-after.png`.
- Fix: a night tint strong enough to read as night, flame/smoke sprites on the stall roofs (an animated tile is
  enough), a few marines visibly on the batey, and a distant drum. Keep the line, but the picture must agree.

### 4. Enemy name labels and damage numbers pile on top of each other in fights with 2+ foes
- Where: every multi-enemy fight: the pier (`?at=yucayeque`), the caney breach (`?at=proof`), Thorne
  (`?at=seacave`), the Trial of Blades once Jaime and Rivera join (`?at=noon`). Worst on the phone.
- What I saw: "Crown Marine B Lv 3" is drawn across Marine A's body; "Captain Rivera Lv 13" across Rael;
  "Governor Thorne" is a label with the boss's body hidden behind the Crown Marine and its label, so the player
  cannot tell which small figure is the boss. Damage digits land on the name ("Rael 1 Lv 13").
  Screens: `yuc-d-07-after.png`, `proof-d-10-after.png`, `thorne-d-03-fight0.png`, `mont-trial-b.png`,
  `mont-trial-c.png`, `pier-l-03-target.png`, `pier-p-02-skill.png`.
- Fix: lay the enemy slots out so labels never overlap (stagger rows with enough vertical gap, or put the name and
  bar under each sprite's feet); float damage numbers above the label, not on it; give the boss a bigger sprite
  or a "boss" frame so it reads as the leader.

### 5. The turn queue cuts names and shows "B > A", which reads as buttons
- Where: top right of every fight. `?at=yucayeque` (pier): "Next: Yari > sergeant > Gianna > B > A > Yari";
  caney: "Musketee > lieutena"; Thorne: "Musketee".
- Screens: `yuc-d-07-after.png`, `proof-d-10-after.png`, `thorne-d-05-fight60.png`.
- Why it hurts: the battle prompts all say "A to confirm, B to go back", so "B > A" looks like an instruction.
  Cut names ("lieutena") look like bugs.
- Fix: show short readable names ("Marine A", "Marine B", "Lieutenant", "Musketeer"), or small portraits/icons in
  the queue; never single letters. If space is short, show four entries instead of six.

### 6. Keyboard players are told "A" and "B"; on a keyboard A walks left
- Where: every prompt: the battle tutorial ("Tap them, or use the arrows and A; B goes back"), target line
  ("A to confirm, B to go back"), item showcase ("A or tap to continue"), chapter card ("A to continue").
  `src/input/keyboard.ts` maps KeyA to left and KeyZ/Enter to confirm. Desktop.
- Screens: `boar-d-01-tut1.png`, `boar-d-08-skilltarget.png`, `end-d-01-card.png`.
- Why it hurts: the owner plays on Windows; a grandparent pressing the A key moves the cursor left and thinks the
  game is stuck. The opening tutorial also lists three devices at once ("Arrows, WASD or the D-pad ... Hold Shift
  or B to run"; "Z or A to talk").
- Fix: name the buttons for the last device used (keyboard: "Z", "X"; pad: "A", "B"; touch: "tap", "B"), one
  helper used by every prompt. Or, simplest, make the A key confirm too (and drop WASD's A for left), but the
  per-device label is the cleaner fix.

### 7. Phone text is very small, and in landscape the picture does not use the screen
- Where: phone portrait 390x844 and landscape 844x390, every screen.
- What I saw: portrait: the picture is 390x220 under a large minimap; dialogue text is about 7 px tall and battle
  labels about 5 px (`open-p-03-tutorial.png`, `pier-p-02-skill.png`). Landscape: the picture is drawn at about
  1.7x (544x306) though 2x (640x360) fits the height; battle labels are about 9 px (`open-l-03-tutorial.png`,
  `pier-l-03-target.png`).
- Fix: in landscape, use the largest zoom that fits the height and let the stick and buttons overlap the picture's
  edges (they are translucent already). In portrait, put the picture at the top at full width, shrink or hide the
  minimap, and make the "Turn your phone sideways" hint a big one-time card rather than small grey text.

### 8. People stand inside each other in staged scenes
- Where: the pier fisher scene (`?at=yucayeque`, `yuc-d-04-talk8.png`, `yuc-d-06-talk16.png`); the Crown captain
  and his marines in the caney, six soldiers in one stacked column overlapping the party (`taken-d-16-talk33.png`);
  the lieutenant and marine merged (`proof-d-08-talk24.png`); Rael on top of Gianna when he takes the Locket
  (`mont-trial-c.png`); halberdiers and Azzy stacked on the forge stairs (`mont-trial-d.png`); the festival crowd
  walking into the party and the fountain (`open-l-05-walked-zoom.png`, `intro-d-10-after.png`); the Echo cane
  worker standing in Patricio (`cane-d-07-talk4.png`).
- Fix: stage groups in a line or a half-circle with one free tile between rows (a row directly in front hides
  the row behind); keep crowd walkers off the party's tile and the tiles next to it, and off props such as the
  fountain; when two people talk face to face vertically, put the speaker beside, not in front.

### 9. "Light pours out of Anissa" is narrated with no effect on her
- Where: the lamp, `?at=lamp-unlit`, zetwal-temple. Desktop.
- What I saw: the lamp lights and the two lanterns glow, but Anissa (hidden behind Gianna, see 1) has no glow, no
  rays, no change. Screens: `lamp-d-04-talk10.png`, `lamp-d-05-talk15.png`.
- Fix: a pulsing light on Anissa (the follower glow already exists: `follower.glowing`), rays to the lamp, the
  screen washing pale gold for a beat; Gianna turns to face her on "Look at me."

### 10. The fisher's look does not match his lines
- Where: Yucayeque pier, `?at=yucayeque`. Desktop.
- What I saw: the sergeant says "Out of my way, old man." The Fisher's portrait and sprite are a young woman with
  long hair and a headband. Screen: `yuc-d-04-talk8.png`.
- Fix: give the Fisher an old man's look (white hair, a net) or change the line.

### 11. Skill numbers mean two different things
- Where: battle Skill menus. Gianna's spells show "Sunthread 3" (3 = SP cost); the weapon-moves tutorial says
  "the number beside one is the turns left". Same position, two meanings. Screens: `boar-d-07-skills.png`,
  `boar-d-10-next.png`.
- Fix: write "3 SP" for costs and "(2 turns)" or a clock icon for cooldowns.

### 12. HP and SP columns have no labels; Spark, Surge, MARK are never explained
- Where: the battle party panel and the Spark box (all fights).
- What I saw: two bars and two numbers per fighter with no "HP"/"SP" heading; the Spark box at the left edge
  fills and says "Surge!" but no tutorial page covers it (the three pages cover turns, Attack/Skill and
  Guard/Item; Swap is not covered either); a purple "▼MARK" appears beside SP during the Trial, partly under the
  selection border. Screens: `boar-d-04-commands.png`, `mont-trial-b.png`, `mont-trial-c.png`.
- Fix: small "HP" and "SP" headings; a fourth tutorial page for Spark/Surge and Swap the first time each matters;
  status tags as an icon plus a one-line explanation when the cursor rests on the fighter.

### 13. Battles indoors and in a sea cave use an outdoor sunset backdrop
- Where: the caney breach (inside the caney), the Thorne blockade (`xaragua-seacave`).
- Screens: `proof-d-10-after.png`, `thorne-d-03-fight0.png`.
- Fix: an interior (posts, thatch) backdrop for the caney and a cave/sea-cave backdrop for Thorne, chosen by the
  map the fight starts on.

### 14. Mixed art scale on the battlefield
- Where: every fight with Crown troops.
- What I saw: the party are large detailed figures; the marines, musketeers and Governor Thorne are small chibi
  sprites. The Crown Musketeer is the Crown Marine with a different label (no musket), so "who is who" fails.
  Screens: `yuc-d-07-after.png`, `thorne-d-03-fight0.png`.
- Fix: battle art for the Crown troops at the party's scale, with the musketeer holding a musket and Thorne
  clearly a governor (coat, hat, larger).

### 15. Twins in the Al-Doria bazaar
- Where: `?at=aldoria`, aldoria-bazaar, desktop.
- What I saw: two women in the same gold headdress and teal dress overlapping each other at the centre; three
  traders with the same white cap and tan robe with green collar. Screen: `ald-d-08-bazaar.png`.
- Fix: run the same no-twins rule the festival crowd now uses over every town's townsfolk (Ardencia and Al-Doria
  included), and keep two townsfolk from standing on adjacent vertical tiles.

### 16. Too many sparkles in some rooms
- Where: the guild vault (`?at=vault-echo`), about ten sparkles on one screen; the forge Echo.
- Screen: `vault-d-01-start.png`.
- Why it hurts: the sparkle means "not used yet"; ten at once tells a new player nothing about where to go.
- Fix: sparkle only things the current objective or an open side quest needs; let flavour objects show their name
  label on approach instead.

### 17. The menu's top line is cryptic, and quest-reward gear is not equipped
- Where: menu (Escape/M), Status tab, `?at=shutters`. Desktop.
- What I saw: "Gold 314 Bag 10/24 Armies 0 Concord 50 / Influence 0/1 Wealth 0/0 Fear 0/0 (Yucayeque/realm)";
  nothing explains Concord, Armies or the "x/y (Yucayeque/realm)" form. The Sun-stitched charm (a quest reward
  whose card says "In battle it heals a little each turn") sits in the bag; the Charm slot shows "-".
  Screen: `occ-d-04-menu.png`.
- Fix: one-line help on the cursor for each top-line value (or a "?" row); when a gear reward fills an empty slot,
  offer "Equip it now?" on the reward card.

### 18. Echo extras look unfinished
- Where: the forge Echo (forge-master), the vault Echo (Yusuf, Caona portraits). Desktop.
- What I saw: faceless or near-faceless sprites and portraits next to the detailed main cast, made harder to read
  by the sepia. Screens: `mont-azzy.png`, `mont-vault.png`.
- Fix: give named Echo characters (Caona above all, she is a founder) real faces; raise contrast in the sepia
  filter a little.

### 19. Small text slips
- "The ferry coach bumps against the pier at dawn" (Chapter 2 arrival, `?at=yucayeque`): they came on a ship;
  "ferry coach" reads as a mistake. Suggest "The ship's boat bumps against the pier".
- "harbour" in Al-Doria (Yari, Yusuf, Caona) versus "harbor" in Chapters 1 and 2 (Yari herself says "harbor"
  there). Pick one spelling for the narration and the party's own lines.
- The minimap place name on desktop is tiny and wraps ("Cenizal: The / Cinder Flats").

### 20. The voyage picture is flatter than the rest
- Where: the voyage after the Chapter 1 card. Desktop.
- What I saw: a flat green strip, a brown bar for the pier, a rowboat of faceless dots drawn over the ship's hull.
  Screen: `end-d-02-after1.png`.
- Fix: the party's map sprites in the boat, the boat beside (not over) the ship, a drawn Pòdlimyè shore.

### 21. Dev only: `?at=` replays every queued toast and an old reward card on arrival
- Where: any `?at=` jump (seen at Yucayeque, the caney, the Trial, the forge, the bazaar, the vault).
- What I saw: "Heard the debate: +1 Influence", a string of "Quest complete: ..." toasts and the Chapter 1
  "Sun-stitched charm" reward card pop up at the first free moment after the jump, over dramatic scenes (Rael's
  reveal, Anaco's capture). Screens: `end-d-04-after3.png`, `taken-d-04-won.png`, `mont-trial-c.png`,
  `mont-vault.png`.
- This muddies the owner's own testing. In normal play I could not confirm whether toasts also wait until a
  scene ends: "Yari reached level 6!" did appear in the middle of Anaco being taken (`taken-d-16-talk33.png`),
  which normal play would show too.
- Fix: clear the toast and showcase queues when a jump finishes; and in normal play, hold level-up and reward
  toasts until a staged scene ends.

## Checked and fine
- B/Escape steps back one level in battle (target -> commands; Skill list -> commands), and the acting fighter
  is outlined and listed in the party panel.
- The intro film: Skip button visible; B skips to the title; the title waits for A.
- The festival crowd has no exact twins; townsfolk tones match each land in what I saw.
- Only Gianna, Anissa and one companion walk the map in Chapters 1 and 2; Azzy plus a companion in Al-Doria.
- Marines and Rael walk in and act their entrances; Anaco's capture is staged on the map.
- The Game Over screen (goddess) reads well and offers a retry.
- No console errors in any run (only software-WebGL warnings from the headless browser).

## Suggested order of work
1 (follower placement), 2 and 3 (shown-not-told), 4 and 5 (battle labels and queue), 6 (button names), 7 (phone
zoom), then 8 to 21.
