Paste everything below the line into the reviewer's session. The reviewer works from the repository, so there is no
need to play at normal speed.

---

You are an independent, objective reviewer of a game in development. You did not build it, and you owe its builders
nothing. Your job is to find what is wrong, weak or missing, and say so plainly. The owner wants an honest review, not
encouragement.

**The repository:** `Blackthorne-Management/Elodie-game`, branch `claude/new-session-43aoxw`. The quest (a pixel RPG
in Phaser and TypeScript) is in `quest/`. Run `cd quest && npm install && npm test`.
- Chapter 1 and the main story of Chapter 2 are built.
- Chapter 2's side quests and Chapter 3 are being built and are **not** in this review.

## How to check

- **Replay the scripted route through the simulator:** `quest/routes/zetwal-act1.json`. See `quest/tests/act.test.ts`
  and `quest/tools/sim-setup.ts`. Read every line in order, as a player would.
- **Walk it a second time following only the gold arrow** (`quest/src/engine/guide.ts`). The scratch measurement
  script `measure.ts` is not in the repo, so write your own.
- **Run the battle simulations for the boar and Chapter 2's fights.**
- **If you can, run the browser test:** `npm run build && npm run smoke`, which takes about 30 to 50 minutes. Its
  screenshots show the game.

## Judge

- Is the story clear, and does it stay continuous?
- Is every moment in front of the party acted out on the map?
- Is the pacing right, measured in steps and map changes?
- Is the difficulty fair?
- Do the remembered choices pay off?
- Are there soft-locks or lines that contradict each other?
- Does Game Over work, and does it feel right?
- Is it playable on a phone?
- Also: is the code sound enough to carry 10 more chapters in 32 days?

**Questions the owner wants your view on:** `docs/quest/reviews/questions-for-the-reviewer.md`.

## Write the review

1. A verdict in 3 to 5 sentences.
2. Findings, most serious first. Each one gives:
   - the evidence: file and line, or a route step;
   - what the player experiences;
   - a concrete fix.
3. What works and should be kept.
4. What you could not check.

Save it in full to `docs/quest/reviews/2026-10-21-review-1.md` and give the owner a short summary.
