## How to check

- **Replay the scripted route through the simulator** (`quest/routes/zetwal-act1.json`, `quest/tests/act.test.ts`,
  `quest/tools/sim-setup.ts`), reading Chapters 3 to 5 line by line as a player would.
- **Walk Chapters 1 to 5 by the gold arrow alone** (`quest/tests/goldWalk.test.ts` now does this; check it is honest,
  and write your own walker if you doubt it).
- **Battle simulations** (`quest/tools/battle-sim.ts`) of every scripted fight in Chapters 2 to 5, counting falls
  across the whole party, not only the active fighters. In the last browser run the smoke bot lost Chapter 4's
  `qasim_vault` twice before winning it: is that fight fair?
- **The level curve:** `node --experimental-strip-types tools/level-curve.ts` (route, played, wanderer lines).
- **If you can, the browser test:** `npm run build && npm run smoke` (about 60 minutes through Chapter 5);
  `SMOKE_FROM=chapter2-end npm run smoke` starts from a save at Chapter 2's end.

## Judge

- Is the story clear and continuous from Chapter 2 into Chapters 3 to 5? Do the families feel like people?
- Is every moment in front of the party acted out on the map?
- Pacing in steps and map changes per objective; any new fetch loops like the old occupied city?
- Is the difficulty fair after the levels pass? Is any boss a wall that the start tip does not explain?
- Soft-locks, saves in awkward places, side quests left dangling, lines that contradict each other.
- Is it still playable on a phone?
- Throughput: seven more chapters and the epilogue in 31 days. What should be cut or simplified first?

**Questions the owner wants your view on:** `docs/quest/reviews/questions-for-the-reviewer.md`.

1. A verdict in 3 to 5 sentences.

   - a concrete fix.
3. What works and should be kept.
4. What you could not check.
