## 1. Elodie, the goddess

The board game's rule holds here too: **Elodie is never shown with a full face**, only as a vast, serene presence:
glowing hands, an eye, or a silhouette woven of stars and gold light (`docs/art/CARD-PROMPTS.md`). She wove the realm
and remembers everything; she does not rule. No sprite or dialogue portrait: she never speaks.

**1a. Key illustration** (16:9; the intro's first shot and the title screen)
```
Illustration for a fantasy adventure game: the goddess Elodie, who wove the world and remembers everything in it. Show her only as a vast, serene presence in a night sky: a tall silhouette woven of stars and threads of gold light, her open hands holding a loom of glowing golden threads that fall down to a sea and the islands and lands of a realm far below. Her face is never shown: where a face would be there is only soft light and a single calm eye of gold light, half closed, watching. Warm gold, cream and deep night blue, with small points of warm light on the lands below like lanterns. Hand-painted fantasy storybook style, crisp shapes, rich but simple, readable when shrunk very small. Wide 16:9 landscape format, one continuous painting. No text, no letters, no frame, no border, no people on the ground in close view, not photorealistic, no anime style.
```

**1b. The Gaze emblem** (square; the Temple's banners, the Gaze lamp, the title screen)
```
A simple emblem for a fantasy temple: a gold sun with twelve straight rays, and in its centre a calm, half-closed eye drawn with a few clean lines, the eye's pupil a small dark circle. Flat gold and cream on a deep blue circle, a thin gold ring around it. Bold, simple, symmetrical shapes that still read at 32 x 32 pixels. Square image, the emblem centred and filling most of it, on a solid flat magenta background (#FF00FF). No text, no letters, no shading gradients, no extra decoration.
```

She is a Haitian-inspired Caribbean stateswoman of about 45, head of an island nation and its diplomat: tall, upright and graceful, composed, warm with her children and guarded with everyone else. Deep brown skin, dark hair in a high, neat bun, warm dark eyes, a calm, intelligent face, a faint sun-shaped birthmark near her temple. Outfit: a long gold gown with a cream sash across the waist, cream sleeves, a simple gold circlet with a single small red stone on her brow, small gold earrings, and a long cream travelling cloak clasped with a gold sun brooch. Show her full body three times on one sheet: front view, side view and back view, standing with her hands lightly folded in front, same scale, evenly spaced, plus one small close-up of her face. Style: hand-painted fantasy storybook illustration matching the attached image, crisp clean outlines, bright warm saturated colours (gold, cream, a touch of red, warm brown skin), well-lit faces, soft shading, clear readable shapes.
