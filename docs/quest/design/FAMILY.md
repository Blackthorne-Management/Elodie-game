# The families: roles in the quest (suggestions, Oct 21)

The owner's direction (Oct 21, `STORY.md`, "the families are bigger"):
- every member of both families is **bumped into along the way** and **helps in a quest or side quest**;
- Azzy calls Rael's siblings *Titi* or *Tío*;
- Gigi and Anissa call Coralie *Tati Coco*.

These are Claude's suggestions, one per person. They are based on each person's parents and homeland, and on where the
story needs help. **The owner hasn't described their personalities yet, so each suggestion's personality is a guess
for the owner to correct.** The suggestions are gone through one by one with the owner. Each line moves to
"settled" when the owner answers, and the build uses the suggestion until then.

Every role below is shown on the map (owner rule): they walk in, help and walk off.

**Built in Chapter 3 (Oct 21):** Zelia (the audience, the records room, the way to old Benny), Amanda (the Lower Town's
kitchen in Ar-1, the tunnel's far end), Joaquin (the guns on the wall), old Benny and Nancy (the quiet rooms, Ar-2's
last step). Azzy calls them Titi Amanda and Tío Joaquin.

**Built in Chapter 4 (Oct 22):** Coralie, "Tati Coco", in Bab al-Dhahab's cloth merchants' row (met on the way along the
A-1 stalls; she sees the Locket before she sees the girls: "That is Papa's sun."), counts the carpet's knots at A-1's
carpet stall, says goodbye when A-1 is reported to Samira and walks down to the quay with her indigo, before the vault.
Her hook for Chapter 5: "Eightgate. Someone has to dress the Regent's guests for this Parley." **Claude's call (review
5.1, to confirm with the family placements):** Gianna asks her "Did you know? About Mother. And Lord Rael." and she
deflects with love: "That is your mother's to tell, chérie. Not mine. She'll be at Eightgate. Make her tell you." So
Coralie knows, and Chapter 5's confrontation stays Elodie's. Azzy does not know her (she is Elodie's side).

**Built in Chapter 5 (Oct 22):** Coralie in Eightgate, continued from Chapter 4 as built (she knows, and deflects). She
comes out of her painted door in the Envoys' Quarter to the sisters ("Gold. I said gold when you were four..."; to
Anissa, held a moment longer: "There you are."), remembers the bazaar ("You found her, then. Did she tell you?" /
"Not yet." / "She will. Not in that Hall."), plans the seamstresses for Keeper Hollis's robes, tells Azzy once that she
made "his" wedding coat while Gianna is at the mirror, talks the Archive's chain off its Sealbearer ("the door could
count to two but not to three"), fits Hollis while the sisters read the page, stays pinning in the fitting room while
Elodie speaks, and waves them off at the Iron Gate. Every line that turns on whether she knows is in one object,
`COCO` in `quest/src/data/dialogue/seat.ts`, so the owner's answer to the question being asked now changes those lines
only (and, if she does not know, she leaves the fitting room before Elodie speaks). Elodie meets Azzy once:
"Azriel." (Claude's call, review 5.1.)

**Built in Chapter 7 (Oct 22):** Kayla at her desk in the Lantern Library, met as the party walks up to it. She gives
K-2 (the Spy Ledger), and at its end hands Gianna **Sayo's house glyphs**, a paper card (`item.kayla_glyphs`): the
file house's screens and Sayo's spy map are marked in them, so she helps decode both without leaving the library, as
the settled line says (the second review's 3.4). After the Fox she reads the Fog Files: "Four lines, four prices. The
names were cut before it reached the Fox. Somebody knew the list would be sold." / "A closed hand. It is not a Crown
seal, and it is not a house's." / "Ardencia's counting-house hand. Kirinoura has copied enough of it to know it."
Her one line about Rael reads both ways: "He writes to me twice a year. He never says anything. That is how I know he
is still himself." **Claude's calls (review 3.4):** no Alcázar backstory, no "say his name", no Rodriguez surname
("You are family." / "I am family who is a clerk. Both are written."), and she never names the Quiet Crown; a test
holds her lines to the locked rule. Haru is not her kin.

**Built in Chapter 8 (Oct 22):** Aiyana on the dyers' roof in the Lamp Bazaar, under a mirror's bracket with a wrench,
the Court's engineer of the great flame ("Whatever burns, I keep"). Azzy crosses the roof, takes the wrench and says
"Titi Aiyana"; Kayla wrote ahead ("Three lines, which for Kayla is a lot"). She opens the way through the Dying Flame:
she gets the party to the Court at noon, opens the chain tower's gate at the night lamp's hour, unbolts the roof
mirrors and keeps the rooftops' ladder, and stands by the last bracket while Anissa holds the disc. **Claude's calls
(the second review's 3.5):** she says only what she measures (the flame's height, the mirrors turned down, the oil);
never Cenizal, never the Locket, never a word about her brother or the thumbprints; at the lit lamp she "looks at
Gianna for a long moment, and does not ask"; the rod line is cut. A test holds her lines to the locked rule. One line
to read with the owner's ear: "Azriel. You've got your grandfather's hands."

**Built in Chapter 9 (Oct 22):** **Jordyn** (Rahel's daughter, Azzy's Titi) is captain of the Regent's watch in
Kingsmere. She meets the party in the hall, makes her offer in the close at the bell (the keep's watch is hers on the
recital's night; the postern's hour), arrests them herself when the Almoner catches them ("Under arrest, Almoner. Not
under you."; "Mother's jaw. I said."), testifies in the Assize, reads the Almoner's commission ("Under the Throne's seal.
No hand."), lays her captain's knot on the clerk's table and walks out with six of the watch. At dawn she returns the
evidence by the book, sends her company by the moor road to the Muster ("And I'll bring Mother. And her mother.") and
says of Rael only: "He wrote to me once in twenty years. One line: 'Keep the law.' I have." **Sebastian
Jean-Philippe** (Elodie's brother; the bill on the heath: "that's Grandpapa's name") is the organist to three courts:
heard before he is seen at St. Agatha's ("I'm your uncle Sebastian."), plays the Agatha Toccata (twice, the slow

neither says a word about anyone's goodness, the arrows or the thumbprints; a test holds them to it. The reviewer
prompt asks for a human read of every Jordyn, Sebastian and Almoner line.

**Built in Chapter 10 (Oct 22):** **Rahel** comes up the long pier with Jordyn's company and her mother. In the Limyè

**Built in Chapter 11 (Oct 22):** after the Turn, the family speaks. **Rael**, by lamplight on the pier: "Your mother
and I married twenty-five years ago, in the year I was given the name you know me by."; to Azzy (Trust): "You have

son. He was always his own. He never explained himself to me either." (not "his mother's": Rael's mother is Rahel, and
Nancy is beside him). **Nancy** takes Anissa's hand ("Child. There are hurt people on this island and a lamp to hold.
Show me where.") and walks the wounded up to the Song House; to Gianna: "Zelia will want to hear that you ate
something." **Joaquin** counts Free Brasador's wounded ("Nobody from the wall."). Patricia's "Count them at dusk.
Harbors lie." becomes the Journal's flank step and the boy's count from the lookout. **Settled (Claude's call, Oct 22):**
STORY's private line, "My father kept his oath and it cost him everything", stands; young Benny's passive (Chapter 3)

**Built in Chapter 12 and the Epilogue (Oct 22):** **Elodie** at last: in her rooms, of Rael's testing, "No. He tested
