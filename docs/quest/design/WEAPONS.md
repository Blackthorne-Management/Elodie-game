# Weapons: "X of Y" (generated from quest/src/data/weapons.ts)

Owner decisions (Oct 15): three designs per rarity per weapon type; the design is the picture, the suffix ("of Y") is what that copy does; every named weapon is a fixed item placed by hand (shops, chests, rewards). Common and uncommon suffixes add stats, rare ones bring a skill (free, no spell slot), epic ones a skill and a special. Legendary stays the relic slot (the secret items).

**Placement:** only the weapons already in Chapter 1 are placed (the forge and the start gear, ids unchanged). The rest are placed chapter by chapter; this list is for the owner to review first.
