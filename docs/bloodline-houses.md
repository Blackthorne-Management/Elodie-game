# THRONE OF BLOODLINES — THE EIGHT HOUSES
### Full lore, homelands, and Generation I-IV ability text

This is the standalone reference flagged in Rulebook Section 12. Each house below replaces its original design codename (kept in parentheses on first mention for continuity with earlier design notes) with its full cultural identity: homeland, backstory, and complete generation-by-generation abilities. Mechanics and numbers are unchanged from the original design; only names and flavor text have been added.

---

## House Brasador (originally "Ashborn")
**Homeland:** Ardencia, a scorched volcanic frontier
**Culture:** Spanish-inspired
**Identity:** Fear/aggression, Early-favored

Ardencia is a land of fire-cracked highlands and old wounds. House Brasador rose from conquistador-warlords who carved out their territory through open conquest, leaving behind a reputation that outlived every war they fought. They do not need to be present to be feared; the brand alone is enough.

| Gen | HP | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | 3 | Basic attack. Passive **Sangre Ardiente** (Burning Blood): +1 Fear on kill. |
| II | 4 | **La Marca** (The Brand): mark an enemy, deal +1 damage to marked targets. |
| III | 4 | Passive upgrades to +2 Fear on kill. **Grito de Guerra** (War Cry): target player skips their next Fear-gain action. |
| IV | 4 | **Reinado de Cenizas** (Reign of Ashes): once per game, instantly gain Fear equal to your total kills this session. |

**Soft downside:** by Gen III-IV, Brasador is visibly the most-Feared house on the table. Other players gain a small bonus specifically when attacking them, representing every rival house's incentive to bring down the reputation everyone fears most.

## House Dorini (originally "Hollowmere")
**Homeland:** Al-Doria, where Mediterranean sea-trade meets desert caravan routes
**Culture:** Mediterranean/Middle-Eastern-inspired
**Identity:** Wealth/economy, Late-favored

Al-Doria is a sprawling entrepôt, half port city and half caravanserai, where spice and silk change hands as often as crowns. House Dorini built their power on ledgers and bazaars rather than armies, converting coin into influence or fear through bribery and hired blades when the need arises.

| Gen | HP | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | 2 | Basic attack. Passive **Baraka Dorini**: +1 Wealth from trade actions. |
| II | 2 | **Daftar al-Dorini** (The Ledger): once per turn, convert 2 Wealth into 1 Influence or 1 Fear. |
| III | 2 | Passive upgrades to +2 Wealth from trade actions. |
| IV | 2 | **Bazaar al-Dhahab** (Golden Bazaar): spend 5 Wealth to instantly gain a full extra action this turn. |

## House Ironvow, of Skarragol (fused culture; mechanical identity unchanged)
**Homeland:** Skarragol, born when fjord-raiders who sailed inland met steppe horse-lords who rode outward
**Culture:** Fused Nordic/Mongolian-inspired
**Identity:** Mobility/aggression, Early-favored

Skarragol's warbands prize one thing above all else: speed. Their oath-bound clans strike before anyone can organize a response, whether on longship or on horseback, and the house's name, Ironvow, is the blood-oath every raider swears before riding or sailing out.

| Gen | HP | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | 3 | Basic attack. Passive **Ironvow's Oath**: movement costs -1 to adjacent territories. |
| II | 3 | **Raider's Charge**: move and attack in the same action. |
| III | 3 | Passive upgrades: also -1 to attack activation cost. |
| IV | 3 | **The Great Raid** (Warpath): once per game, take two full turns in a row. Using this forces you to reveal your full hand for that double-turn. |

## House Suzumori (originally "Duskrattle")
**Homeland:** Kuroshi, a fog-veiled island nation
**Culture:** Japanese-inspired
**Identity:** Information/control, Mid-favored

Kuroshi's power has never come from open strength. House Suzumori built a network of spies, informants, and whispers reaching into every court on the board, listening at keyholes and pulling strings long before anyone realizes the game has already turned against them.

| Gen | HP | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | 2 | Basic attack. Passive **Kagemimi** (Shadow's Ear): view one player's hand once per "day" phase. |
| II | 2 | **Sasayaki Ha** (Whispering Blade): force a player to discard one card of your choice. |
| III | 2 | Passive upgrades: view a hand AND one resource pool total (Influence/Fear/Wealth breakdown) of any player. |
| IV | 2 | **Ayatsuri** (Marionette's Thread): once per game, force another player to redirect their next action. |

## House Vai'tama (originally "Wraithmoor")
**Homeland:** Moa'olani, a scattered reef archipelago
**Culture:** Native-islander-inspired
**Identity:** Adaptive/chaotic, swingy at all stages

Moa'olani's people believe the drowned ancestors never fully leave. House Vai'tama's warriors are said to carry the fallen within them in battle, inheriting strength, memory, and secrets from the dead with every generation.

| Gen | HP | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | 2 | Basic attack. Passive **Tahu'ora**: on death, your heir's inherited Legacy trait can be drawn from ANY dead bloodline, not just your own. |
| II | 2 | **Ancestor's Mask** (Borrowed Face): once per game, copy another player's currently active passive for one full turn. |
| III | 2 | Passive upgrades: draw 2 Legacy options on death and pick one. |
| IV | 2 | **Tide of Ancestors** (Legion of the Dead): permanently gain a second passive, copied from any bloodline currently eliminated from the game entirely. |

## House Kay Soley (originally "Goldenreach")
**Homeland:** Zetwal, a sun-bright coastal nation built around a temple to Elodie
**Culture:** Kreyol/Haitian-inspired
**Identity:** Influence/diplomacy, Late-favored

Zetwal's temple-court claims direct descent from Elodie's forgotten mortal children, marked at birth by a faint sun-shaped birthmark said to be her signature. Whether the claim is true or a convenient myth is left open; every other house either envies or resents them for it, and that perceived legitimacy is the whole of their power.

| Gen | HP | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | 2 | Basic attack. Passive **Limyè Elodie** (Elodie's Light): +1 Influence from court/marriage actions. |
| II | 2 | **Sèman Lapè** (Peace Oath): once per game, force a temporary truce with one player (neither can attack the other for 2 turns). |
| III | 2 | Passive upgrades to +2 Influence from court actions. |
| IV | 2 | **Rit Solèy** (Rite of the Sun): the combined resource total you need before you may declare a Claim on the Throne is reduced by 2, for you only. |

## House Agnivansh (originally "Emberfall")
**Homeland:** Jwaladesh, Land of Flame
**Culture:** Indian-subcontinent-inspired
**Identity:** Aggro/tempo, Early-favored

Jwaladesh's warriors follow the fire-god's doctrine: hesitation is the only true death. House Agnivansh, the Fire Lineage, trains for speed above all, striking fast and relentlessly and trusting that momentum itself is a kind of armor.

| Gen | HP | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | 3 | Basic attack. Passive **Agni ki Shakti** (Power of Fire): combat abilities cost 1 less to activate. |
| II | 3 | **Rakt Ki Pyaas** (Thirst for Blood, Blood Frenzy): after landing a kill, take a bonus half-move immediately. |
| III | 3 | Passive upgrades: also gain +1 Fear whenever you win any combat, kill or not. |
| IV | 2* | **Ananta Yuddha** (Endless War): once per game, ignore all activation costs for one full turn. |

*\*Agnivansh has the lowest Gen IV HP ceiling of all 8 houses, their glass-cannon soft downside: devastating output, but the easiest house to finish off once caught.*

## House Stillwater, of Aldermoor
**Homeland:** Aldermoor, a quiet, fog-wrapped moorland
**Culture:** English-inspired
**Identity:** Defensive/patient, Late-favored

Aldermoor's old families have weathered centuries of invasion and famine the same way every time: by simply outlasting everyone else. Their strength has never been conquest. It is endurance, patience, and the quiet certainty that everyone louder than them eventually burns out first.

| Gen | HP | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | 2 | Basic attack. Passive **Stillwater's Patience**: once per game, block/cancel a Grudge Reckoning bonus used against you. |
| II | 2 | **The Long Watch** (Patience): if you take no aggressive action on your turn, gain +1 to any single resource. |
| III | 2 | Passive upgrades: the block/cancel ability recharges once per game instead of being single-use total. |
| IV | 4 | **Aldermoor's Vigil** (The Long Game): your Gen IV character gets +2 max HP on top of normal stats. |
