# Design review, October 13 2026

This is a full pass over the rules, all 126 cards, the houses and the balance, written before the card and rules freeze on Oct 31.
Every fix below is now decided (see the list); section 6 stays open for playtesting.

**Decided so far** (with a second, independent review, going through its questions one at a time):
- The Throne protects only a player eligible to claim (rules-decisions 76).
- At 6+ players no attack deals more than 2 damage (77), with Ironvow's extra movement ending at Gen III (78).
- Interference and Embargo come after a card names its target, and the app shows the card and its target (79).
- 2 players: both Race (houses as usual) and Battle (neighbouring houses) are official; the players choose (82).
- Box and rulebook: 2–8 players · 60–150 min · best with 3–6; about 20 minutes per player; first game with 3 to 5.
- Brasador's La Marca and Kay Soley's Rit Solèy start at Gen I (81).
- Yaguana starts with a Legacy (Ancestor's Voice), Areíto from Gen II; Stillwater's Gen I power is Unbroken (83).
- Everything else in sections 2–5 (84), with the owner's changes: two discard piles (Hand Cards, Instants); lasting cards stay out in the engine; truces counted in turns; Ambush 1 damage; ◎ Target cards; Gambler's Luck max 2; Dodge gains a resource; Counterfeit Coin gains a resource of your choice; Market Day draws 2 keeps 1; Bridge the Gap 5; Hidden Vault 3 with no Wealth; Caravan Passing reworked; B10 dropped.

Sources: two read-only audits (rules text vs decisions vs engine; card text vs engine) and new simulations (5,000 games at 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 players, plus 8,000 more at 6 and 8 players).

## 1. Balance: where it stands

| Check | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Houses (5,000 games) | Every house wins 0.95–1.09× its fair share; χ² 11.5, p 0.12 | Balanced |
| Houses at 8 players | Kay Soley 1.26×, Suzumori 0.80× | Watch; retest after card fixes |
| Seat order | 8,000 games at 6 and 8 players: every seat 0.95–1.06×, p 0.7 | No first-player advantage. Elodie going first costs nothing. |
| Runaway leader | The resource leader after round 4 wins 1.5× fair share (4–8 players). The leader one round before the end wins only 55% at 4 players and 30% at 8 players. | Healthy: leading helps, but the Throne challenge keeps it open |
| Game length | Median 9–11 rounds; 90% of games end by round 15–16 | Good |
| Sudden Death | 6–10% of games (15% at 2 players) | Good |
| Specters | They essentially never win (0.02%) | As designed (they can't claim), but see the open question on downtime |

### Card outliers in the simulation
The simulation compares how often the eventual winner played each card with how often a random player would.
Pure luck Instants (Coin Toss, Fortune's Wheel) sit around 1.3×, because winners survive longer and see more cards.
So 1.3× is the baseline, not 1.0×.

- **46 Bridge the Gap: 1.61×.** It is the strongest card in the deck. It moves up to 8 squares, compared with Swift Steed's 2.
- **70 Gambler's Luck: 1.46×.** It is a free Instant worth about 2.9 Wealth on average, while the other dice Instants are worth about 0.5–1.5.
- **50 Stolen Goods (0.86×) and 54 Uneasy Truce (0.91×)** are the weakest cards that see play.
- **Reactions (58–65, 121–126)** don't appear in this count because they are played as answers, not actions. They were judged on their text instead.

## 4. Smaller wording fixes
- Brasador's downside: "the most Fear" should be "**strictly** the most Fear".
- Agni ki Shakti III: add "(not in a Challenge)".
- Rakt Ki Pyaas: ruling [39] still says "straight line"; the engine and the house text use any path. Mark [39] superseded.
- Yaguana: "any fallen bloodline" should be "any house that has lost a character". Gen III's "draw 2 Legacies" needs a physical method (shuffle the Legacy cards). Areíto is a Gen I rule; reword "new abilities at Gen II, III and IV".
- Dorini: "+1 Wealth once per turn" means once in **each player's** turn. Say so.
- Setup: put the house vote before the lottery. Write "If a player named Elodie (or Lolo/Lodie)…".
- Specter rule: list the Instants a Specter can't pick (Duel of Honor, Cursed Dice, Wildfire, Risky Crossing, Elodie's Sorrow, Exile, Judgment, Trial, Reckoning, Gilded Cage), or print an icon on them.
- Names: use one name per power everywhere (War Cry or Grito de Guerra, not both).
- Rules-decisions: mark [4], [38], [39] and [44] superseded. cards.ts still says "120-card".

## 5. Physical-game gaps
- **Score pad** for kills, Reckonings and fallen houses. It is needed for the Sudden Death tie-break, Reinado de Cenizas, Elodie's Reckoning and Yaguana's Legacy pool.
- **Components list** is missing: shield, La Marca, War Cry and Specter markers, Legacy cards, generation cards, the round marker, and Grudge Token counts (suggest 5 per house = 40).

## 6. Open design questions (no data can answer these; playtest)
1. **Downtime at 8 players.** A round is 8 turns plus reactions. Is waiting fun? Consider a turn timer in the app and a "plan your move while others play" tip.
2. **Specters.** One play per round and no way to win. Is that enough to keep an eliminated player at the table for 5+ more rounds?
3. **Kingmaking.** Late in the game, a player who can't win can still decide who does (challenges, Reckonings). Probably acceptable for this kind of game, but watch for it.
4. **Teaching.** 126 cards with many exceptions. The first game with new players will lean on the app's rule guide.
