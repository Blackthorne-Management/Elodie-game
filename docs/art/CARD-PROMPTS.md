# Card art: 126 prompts

Each block is a complete prompt: paste one per image, in any chat, in any order. **Attach the board painting
(the map you made) to every prompt** as the style reference; that and the shared style line keep 126 pictures
looking like one set.

**What you're making:** the full card artwork, like a trading card. The painting fills the whole card. Later
we lay a PNG overlay on top of every card: the border, a title plate across the top, and a text box over the lower
part. So the art must have **no frame, no border, no title and no text**, and must leave room for those.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Size** | 1000 × 1400 (vertical, 5:7, standard trading-card shape) |
| **Labels** | Every card is tagged `HAND CARD`, `INSTANT` or `ELODIE (Instant)`; that tag says which overlay goes on it (silver, bronze or gold-starred). Cards 1–65 and 121–126 (the Reactions) are Hand Cards; 66–120 are Instants, 10 of them Elodie cards (81, 83, 84, 89, 90, 94, 95, 96, 98, 102). |
| **File name** | `card-<number>.png`, e.g. `card-1.png` for Royal Favor |
| **Layout** | Top 12%: calm background for the title. 15–60%: the main subject. Bottom 40%: the scene continues under the text box, with nothing important in it. |

```
┌────────────────────┐
│  (calm: title)     │  top 12%
│                    │
│   MAIN SUBJECT     │  15–60%
│                    │
│░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░│
│░ scene continues ░░│  bottom 40%: text box goes over this
│░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░│
└────────────────────┘
```

**People on the cards:** every person is described by people, age, gender and clothing, spread across the realm's
eight peoples so the cards don't all show the same two or three faces. Each prompt also spells out what its peoples
look like and wear, so it works on its own in any tool with no memory of earlier prompts. The peoples, by house:

| People | Look and dress |
|---|---|
| Ardencian (Brasador) | Spanish-inspired; olive skin; blackened steel, crimson and black |
| Al-Dorian (Dorini) | Mediterranean / Middle-Eastern-inspired; olive to brown skin; emerald silks, pale robes, headscarves |
| Skarragol (Ironvow) | Nordic and Mongolian steppe; fair to ruddy or steppe features; furs, braids, round shields |
| Suzumori | Japanese-inspired; layered violet and charcoal robes, curved roofs |
| Yaguana | Taíno-inspired (Caribbean islanders); bronze-brown skin, long straight black hair, white cotton wraps, red-ochre and black body paint, shell and gold-copper beads, dugout canoes |
| Kay Soley | Caribbean / Haitian-inspired; dark skin; white, gold and sky blue, gold earrings |
| Jwaladeshi (Agnivansh) | Indian-inspired; brown skin; saffron and crimson, turbans, gilded armour |
| Aldermoor (Stillwater) | English-inspired; pale or freckled skin; wool coats, slate blue and grey |

**Same people for any unequal scene:** wherever one person kneels to, serves, obeys, pays, is taxed, imprisoned,
robbed, punished or given charity by another, both are from the same people. Mixed groups appear only as equals
(allies, rivals, duellists, traders, a crowd). Crime roles are spread so no people is cast as the usual villains.
Every pairing should make sense for the moment shown: fighters are evenly matched, and people meet the way their
station and situation suggest.

If a picture comes back with the wrong people, ask: *"Same picture, but make the person [description from the prompt]."*

**Tips**
- Do a whole category in one chat ("Next one, same style and layout: …") so the look stays steady, and re-attach
  the map when you start a new chat.
- The 10 Elodie cards (marked ✦) show the goddess only as hands, an eye or a starry silhouette, so she never looks
  different from card to card.
- If a picture comes back with text or a frame, ask: *"Same picture, remove all text and any border."*
- If the subject sits too low, ask: *"Same picture, move the subject higher; keep the bottom part simple."*
