# Character art: 32 full-body prompts

One picture per character, 8 houses × 4 generations, painted as a full tarot-size card. The **character card** shows the whole
figure with its own overlay design (name plate and stats panel, added later), and the **pawn on the board** shows a
head-and-shoulders circle cut from the same picture, so the layout below matters.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Card** | Tarot size, 70 × 120 mm (bigger than the game cards, so each player's character stands out on the table) |
| **Size** | 1050 × 1800 (vertical, 7:12) |
| **File name** | `portrait-<house>-<generation>.png`, e.g. `portrait-brasador-1.png` |
| **Layout** | Top 10%: calm backdrop for the name. The whole figure from the top of the head (about 12% down) to the feet (about 68% down). Bottom 30%: ground and backdrop continue under the abilities panel. |
| **Background** | Opaque: a soft painted backdrop of the house's homeland in its colours, filling the card. |

```
┌──────────────┐
│ (calm: name) │  top 10%
│     head     │  ~12%
│              │
│  FULL BODY   │
│              │
│     feet     │  ~68%
│░░░░░░░░░░░░░░│
│░ abilities ░░│  bottom 30%: panel goes over this
│░░░░░░░░░░░░░░│
└──────────────┘
```

The pawn on the board shows a head-and-shoulders circle cut from this same picture, so keep the head where the
layout says.

**How to keep each family consistent**
1. **Attach the board painting** to every prompt (style reference).
2. **Make Generation I of a house first.** For Generations II–IV, also attach that Gen I picture and add: *"A blood
   relative of the person in the second attached image: same family features and colouring, but a different person,
   age and pose as described."*
3. **Keep a house in one chat** while you make its four characters.

Every character is an adult. Each generation is the next heir after the one before dies. The names match the
story game (docs/quest): Gen IV is the present-day hero or companion, Gen II is the ancestor met in the Echo
(rules-decisions 86).
